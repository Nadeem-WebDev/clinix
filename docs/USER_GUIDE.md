# Clinix – User Guide

**Clinic & OPD management software for small clinics (1–5 doctors).**
Patients, appointments, the waiting-room queue, consultations, prescriptions,
billing and reports – in one place, on a computer, tablet or phone.

## 1. What Clinix does

| Area | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Patients** | Register patients (each gets an ID like P-10001), search them, keep their details, history and documents in one profile. |
| **Appointments** | Book, reschedule, cancel and mark no-shows. Every visit gets a token number for the day. |
| **Queue** | See who is waiting and who is with the doctor. Add walk-in patients at the desk. Doctors call the next patient with one click. |
| **Waiting-room screen** | A page for the TV in your waiting room (or a patient's phone) showing the token now being seen – no login needed. |
| **Consultations** | Record complaint, vitals, examination, diagnosis, treatment plan and follow-up date. |
| **Prescriptions** | Add medicines with dose, frequency, timing and duration; print a professional prescription with the doctor's signature. |
| **Billing** | Create bills, take payment (Cash, UPI, Card, Online, Other), print receipts, and refund. Visits the doctor has finished appear in a "Ready to bill" list. |
| **Reports** | Patients, appointments and revenue over any date range, with charts and Excel download. |
| **Documents** | Upload reports, scans and old prescriptions to a patient's profile. |
| **Staff** | Add doctors, receptionists and nurses; switch accounts off when someone leaves. |
| **Audit log** | A record of who did what, and when. |
| **WhatsApp** *(optional)* | Appointment confirmations, reminders and queue updates to patients. |
| **Email** *(optional)* | The same messages by email, sent in your clinic's name – patients can reply to you directly. |

Clinix works on phones and tablets as well as computers, and has light and
dark themes.

## 2. Getting started

1. **Create your clinic.** On the sign-in page choose *Create your clinic*,
   then enter the clinic's details and your own. You become the clinic's
   **Owner**.
2. **Set up the clinic** (*Settings*, Owner only): address, phone and
   contact email, default consultation fee, working hours, time zone, and
   your clinic logo.
   The logo and details appear on every prescription and receipt.
3. **Add your team** (*Staff*): create an account for each doctor,
   receptionist and nurse. Each person signs in with their own email and
   password.
4. **Doctors complete their profile** (account menu → *My doctor profile*):
   qualifications, specialisation, registration number and council, and a
   photo or scan of their signature. These print on prescriptions.
5. **Put the waiting-room screen up** (optional): *Settings* shows your
   clinic's waiting-room link – open it on the TV in your waiting area.

## 3. Who does what – the five roles

| Role | In short |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Runs the clinic. Can do everything, including clinic settings and creating other owners and admins. |
| **Admin** | Manages the clinic day to day. Can do everything the owner can, **except** change clinic settings or create owner/admin accounts. |
| **Doctor** | Sees patients: runs their own queue, records consultations, writes prescriptions, uploads documents. Does not handle bills. |
| **Receptionist** | The front desk: registers patients, books appointments, adds walk-ins, creates bills and takes payment. Does not see medical notes. |
| **Nurse** | Supports the doctor: views patients, appointments and the queue, and can update patient details. Does not see medical notes or bills. |

## 5. A typical patient visit

1. **Front desk** – the patient arrives.
   - *Booked earlier?* Find the appointment and press **Mark arrived**.
   - *Walk-in?* **Queue → Add walk-in**: pick or quickly register the patient
     (name, phone, age, gender) and the doctor. They join the end of the
     queue with the next token number.
2. **Waiting room** – the patient sees their token on the waiting-room
   screen.
3. **Doctor** – presses **Call** in the Queue, then **Consult**.
4. **Doctor** – fills in the consultation and saves. Clinix goes straight to
   the **prescription**; add the medicines and save (or choose *Skip, no
   prescription*). **Finalize** the prescription to sign and lock it, then
   print it for the patient.
5. **Doctor** – presses **Complete** in the Queue.
6. **Front desk** – the patient now appears under **Billing → Ready to
   bill**. Press **Generate Bill** (the consultation fee is filled in), add
   anything else, create the bill, then **Record Payment** and print the
   receipt.

## 6. Features in detail

### Dashboard
Opens when you sign in, and shows what matters for your role.
- **Owner / Admin:** today's appointments, patients (new and returning),
  who is waiting, consultations done, **today's revenue** (payments received
  minus refunds made today), unpaid bills, and follow-ups due this week.
- **Doctor:** your appointments today, who you're seeing now, who's next in
  your queue, and your upcoming follow-ups.
- **Receptionist / Nurse:** today's appointments and who is waiting.
- Quick buttons for the most common tasks of each role.

### Patients
- Register a patient with name, phone, age or date of birth, gender, and
  optional details (address, blood group, allergies, emergency contact…).
  Clinix gives each one a patient ID (P-10001, P-10002…).
- Search by name, phone number or patient ID.
- The patient profile brings together everything about them – visits,
  consultations, prescriptions, appointments, bills, documents and
  follow-ups (what each role sees is in the table above).
- **Visits** lists every time the patient was seen, newest first, with that
  visit's consultation, prescriptions and bills.
- **Follow-ups** lists every follow-up the doctor set, marked *Due today*,
  *Overdue*, *Upcoming* or *Done*.

### Appointments
- Book a patient with a doctor for a date and time. Clinix stops two
  bookings overlapping for the same doctor.
- Filter by Today, Tomorrow or Upcoming, and by doctor or status.
- Reschedule, cancel, or mark a patient as a no-show.
- **Token numbers:** every visit gets a token, one running sequence for the
  whole clinic each day, so no two patients in the waiting room hold the same
  number.

### Queue and walk-ins
- Shows who is waiting and who is with the doctor, in token order. Doctors
  see their own queue; everyone else can filter by doctor.
- **Add walk-in** puts a patient straight into today's queue. If they
  already have a booking today, Clinix offers *Mark arrived instead* so they
  aren't added twice.
- Doctors **Call** the next patient, **Skip** someone who isn't ready (they
  can still be called later), and **Complete** the visit.

### Waiting-room screen
- A page for a TV or a patient's phone, at your clinic's own link (shown in
  *Settings*). It needs no login and refreshes by itself.
- Shows your clinic's name and logo, the token being seen, how many are
  waiting, and a rough wait time.
- **Patients' privacy is protected:** names are shortened (e.g. "Priya S."),
  and no phone numbers or medical details are ever shown.

### Consultations
- Chief complaint, symptoms, vitals (temperature, BP, pulse, breathing rate,
  SpO2, weight, height), examination notes, diagnosis, treatment plan, and a
  follow-up date with instructions.
- Started from the queue, so the patient and doctor are filled in.
- Once the visit is marked complete, its consultation is locked and can no
  longer be changed.

### Prescriptions
- Any number of medicines, each with name, dose, frequency (e.g. 1-0-1),
  route, timing (e.g. after food), duration, quantity and instructions,
  plus general advice.
- **Finalize** locks the prescription as the permanent record.
- **Print / PDF:** a professional A4 prescription with your logo and
  clinic details, the doctor's name, qualifications and registration, the
  patient's details, complaint, vitals and diagnosis, the medicines table,
  advice and follow-up date.
- The doctor's **signature is printed only on finalized prescriptions**. A
  draft shows "Draft – not signed" and a faint DRAFT watermark, so an
  unfinished prescription never looks signed.

### Billing
- Create a bill with any number of items, discount and tax. Totals are
  always calculated by Clinix.
- **Ready to bill:** visits the doctor has completed today that haven't been
  billed yet appear at the top of the Billing page. *Generate Bill* fills in
  the patient, the doctor and the clinic's consultation fee.
- **Take payment** in full or in parts – Cash, UPI, Card, Online or Other.
  The bill shows Unpaid, Partly paid or Paid. A payment can never be more
  than the amount still due.
- Once any payment is recorded, the bill's items are locked, to protect the
  record.
- **Print / PDF:** a professional A4 bill/receipt – titled BILL until
  something is paid, RECEIPT after – with items, totals, the total in words
  ("Rupees One Thousand One Hundred Only"), payments and the status.
- **Refund:** gives back everything paid on a bill, as of today. The refund
  is shown on the bill and receipt, and is taken off revenue on the day it
  happens – earlier days' figures don't change.

### Reports *(Owner and Admin)*
Choose a date range (last 7, 30 or 90 days, or any dates):
- **Patients:** total, new and returning, with new patients per day.
- **Appointments:** totals by status (completed, cancelled, no-show…) and
  by doctor.
- **Revenue:** total and daily average, revenue over time (daily, weekly or
  monthly), and by payment method and by doctor. Revenue is always
  payments received **minus** refunds.
- **Export Excel** downloads a ready-to-use spreadsheet.

### Documents
- Upload lab reports, scans, X-rays or old prescriptions (PDF, JPG or PNG,
  up to 10 MB) to a patient's profile.
- Files are private: each view opens a link that expires shortly after.

### Staff *(Owner and Admin)*
- Add doctors, receptionists and nurses. Only the **Owner** can add other
  owners and admins.
- **Disable** an account when someone leaves – they are signed out at once
  and can't sign in again. Re-enable it any time. Owner and admin accounts
  can't be disabled.
- Open a doctor's **Profile** to fill in their details and signature.

### Doctor profile and signature
- Qualifications, specialisation, registration number and medical council,
  printed on that doctor's prescriptions.
- A signature image (PNG or JPG, up to 1 MB – a clear photo or scan on white
  paper works best).
- Edited by the doctor themselves, or by the owner/admin. The signature is
  private: only that doctor and the owner/admin can see it.

### Clinic settings *(Owner only)*
- Clinic name, address, contact phone and **contact email** (printed on
  prescriptions and bills, and where patients' email replies arrive).
- Default consultation fee (used when billing a visit).
- Working hours for each day of the week.
- **Time zone** – "today", tokens, reports and printed dates all follow your
  clinic's own clock (India time by default).
- **Clinic logo** (PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 2 MB) – shown in the app, on the
  waiting-room screen and on prescriptions and receipts.
- **WhatsApp messages** and **email messages** on or off for your clinic
  (see below).
- Your waiting-room screen link.

### WhatsApp messages *(optional)*
When switched on, patients get WhatsApp messages when an appointment is
booked, a reminder the day before, when they join the queue and when they
are called in, and when their prescription or receipt (once fully paid) is
ready. A patient who replies **STOP** is never
messaged again. This needs a one-time setup with Meta (WhatsApp's owner) by
Clinix support before it can be switched on.

- Messages come from **Clinix's WhatsApp number**, not your clinic's own
  number. Each one names your clinic and ends with your clinic's phone
  number, so patients know who it's from and how to reach you.
- **Replies to the Clinix number are not read** (apart from STOP), and every
  message tells patients so. Patients who want to reschedule should call
  your clinic.
- Make sure your clinic's **phone number is filled in under Settings**
  before switching messages on – that's the number patients are given. (If
  it's empty, messages give your clinic's email, or ask patients to contact
  your front desk.)

### Email messages *(optional)*
When switched on (*Settings → Email notifications*, Owner only), patients
who have an **email address on file** get the same messages as on WhatsApp:
appointment booked, a reminder the day before, when they join the queue and
when they are called in, and when their prescription or receipt (once fully
paid) is ready. You can use email, WhatsApp, or both.

- Emails come **in your clinic's name** ("Sunrise Family Clinic via
  Clinix"), with your logo, and include your clinic's phone number.
- **Patients can reply** – replies go straight to your clinic's contact
  email (fill it in under *Settings → Clinic details*).
- Every email has an **Unsubscribe** link. A patient who uses it isn't
  emailed by your clinic again.
- To include a patient, add their email address when registering them or
  on their profile.
- Email sending is set up once by Clinix support before it can be used.

### Audit log *(Owner and Admin)*
A permanent record of important actions – sign-ins, staff changes, bills,
payments, refunds, prescriptions printed, settings changes and more – with
who did it, when, and from where. Filter by person, action and date.

## 7. Your account

From the account menu (top right):
- **Change password** – you'll need your current password. Your other
  devices are signed out; the one you're using stays signed in.
- **My doctor profile** (doctors only).
- **Sign out.**

The **theme** switch – light, dark, or follow your device – sits in the top
bar on computers and inside the account menu on phones.

**Forgot your password?** Ask your clinic's owner to contact Clinix
support, who will give you a temporary password.

## 8. Privacy and security

- **Each clinic's data is completely separate** – no other clinic can ever
  see your patients.
- **Medical notes stay with clinical staff:** receptionists and nurses
  can't open consultations or prescriptions.
- Passwords are stored encrypted and are never visible to anyone, including
  Clinix support.
- Sign-in attempts are limited, to stop anyone guessing passwords.
- A disabled staff account stops working immediately.
- Everything important is recorded in the audit log.
- Clinix support never sees your patients' names or medical records – only
  counts (how many patients, appointments and so on).

## 9. Good to know

- **Refunds are for the whole bill** – there are no partial refunds yet.
- **Ready to bill shows today's visits only.** A visit not billed by the end
  of the day can still be billed from *Create Bill*.
- Printed amounts use **"Rs."**, and bills don't show a GSTIN yet.
- A follow-up counts as *Done* once the patient is seen again.
- One login belongs to one clinic.
- A doctor who also owns the clinic needs a separate doctor account to see
  patients (only doctor accounts appear in the doctor lists).
